# Salary bands

Approved base salary bands for Halvorsen Robotics:

- L3: $92,000 to $118,000 base salary
- L4: $118,000 to $149,000 base salary
- L5: $149,000 to $188,000 base salary
- L6: $188,000 to $236,000 base salary

Offers above the band midpoint need approval from the department head.

# Unused PTO liability

Field Service: unused PTO days carried over into next year total 40 days. Carry over above the 5 day limit expires, but the unused PTO balance that does carry over is booked as a liability for next year at the average daily salary rate.

Retrofit: unused PTO days carried over into next year total 53 days. Carry over above the 5 day limit expires, but the unused PTO balance that does carry over is booked as a liability for next year at the average daily salary rate.

Platform: unused PTO days carried over into next year total 66 days. Carry over above the 5 day limit expires, but the unused PTO balance that does carry over is booked as a liability for next year at the average daily salary rate.

Perception: unused PTO days carried over into next year total 79 days. Carry over above the 5 day limit expires, but the unused PTO balance that does carry over is booked as a liability for next year at the average daily salary rate.

# Salary bands

Approved base salary bands for Bramblecote Foods:

- L3: $78,000 to $99,000 base salary
- L4: $99,000 to $126,000 base salary
- L5: $126,000 to $160,000 base salary
- L6: $160,000 to $204,000 base salary

Offers above the band midpoint need approval from the department head.

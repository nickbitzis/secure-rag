# Paid time off policy

Employees at Bramblecote Foods accrue 25 days of paid time off per year.

Up to 8 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Days above that limit expire on December 31.

Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for absences longer than three days.
